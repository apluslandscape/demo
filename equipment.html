Equipment

What the business owns and leases, and what each item still needs

This register is the record of every vehicle and every major piece of equipment the business owns, leases or rents. It holds what each one is, what it is worth, whether its registration is current, what paperwork we hold for it, and whether the insurer has confirmed it is on the policy.

Where the register does not yet know something, it says Unknown, and those are the only entries that create work. Where someone has established that there is genuinely nothing to record — a machine that never carried a serial number, for instance — it says None, confirmed, with who settled it and when, so the same question does not come round again.

Nothing is recorded as rented

Rented items belong here for as long as they are on site, so that they come off the insurance policy when they go back. Two recent rentals — a lift kept for a single day, and a wheel loader with its buckets — stayed on cover after they were returned, because there was nowhere to record that they had gone.

Nothing is recorded as sold

Sold items are never deleted; they stay here, marked sold, and they stop appearing on the insurance schedule. The spreadsheet has no column for this, which is how a machine sold about three years ago is still on the insurer's list today.

Nothing is recorded as written off

An item destroyed or scrapped is marked here rather than removed, so the record of what was owned, and when, stays complete.

Why this matters

On the spreadsheet, this item's serial cell reads DOES NOT HAVE A VIN OR SERIAL #. That is the right answer written in the only place there was to write it.
